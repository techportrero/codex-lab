## Business Context

Leaders needed answers that depended on data spread across several systems, each with different structures, definitions, and refresh expectations. Reporting existed, but teams lacked confidence in consistency and timeliness.

## Problem

Disconnected data created competing versions of the truth. Analysts and managers spent too much time assembling information and too little time interpreting what it meant or what action to take.

## My Role

I helped define the reporting model from both the business and technical sides: clarifying KPIs, tracing data lineage, aligning definitions with stakeholders, and shaping the workflow needed to produce usable analytics.

## Systems Involved

The work connected operational platforms with a warehouse and BI layer, creating a bridge between system transactions and executive-level reporting.

## Technical Approach

I focused on data mapping, transformation logic, metric definitions, and the reporting experience itself. The emphasis was not just building a dashboard, but making sure the numbers reflected real business meaning and could be trusted by decision-makers.

## Outcome

The resulting analytics flow reduced manual effort, improved reporting consistency, and gave teams a stronger basis for identifying trends, exceptions, and next actions.
